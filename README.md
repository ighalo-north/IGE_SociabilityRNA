# IGE_SociabilityRNA
RNA analysis of indirect genetic effects of sociability in Drosophila melanogaster

Heritable variation among group members can affect the social phenotype of a focal individual
One’s phenotype is modified by the indirect genetic effects (IGEs) of group members. Such IGEs can influence the rate and direction of evolution. The mechanisms underlaying IGEs on social phenotypes, however, are mostly unknown, and we are in a unique position to address this important knowledge gap.
Prediction: there will be a significant difference between the two treatments in focal flies’ gene expression. The genes expressed in stimulus flies will be more similar than expected by chance to the ones identified in (Torabi-Marashi et al. 2025). This project aims to verify our predictions regarding the indirect genetic effects of sociability in Drosophila melanogaster.
RNA sequencing reads from (12 individuals x 2 sexes x 2 treatments x 8 days) = 32 samples
- data from day 5 was discarded due to quality

Illumina Nextera adapters
Illumina NovaSeq PE100 1500M reads
